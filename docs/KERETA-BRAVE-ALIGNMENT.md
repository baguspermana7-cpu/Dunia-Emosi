## Immersion checklist per level (owner: "immersive ada vfx micro interaction dan semuanya")

  dust (24), soft gold (26–27).
- Every story beat has a VFX moment (shared library) AND a micro-interaction: the cast reacts when Linus arrives
  (pose swap, small hop), Linus's face changes (happy/sad sprites), the caboose wobbles, birds lift off when he
  passes, the searchlight sweeps, a wreck creaks.
- Cast only where the scenario puts them (Scarlet never at the bridge; Henry not in the logging levels).
- Text short; the story card before each level states the scenario in one line.
