## Kid-safe adaptations (fixed, use everywhere)
- James: never a gun. Shown with his scroll / pointing (art: james/point-scroll, walk-scroll); his "threat" in sc19 =
  he points and shouts from the slope, a flash of his LANTERN, Linus keeps going.
- Carter: never chain or whip. Sitting on the log load with hands on hips / pointing (carter/hands-hips, point);
  the "chain" = a big COUPLING HOOK + latch between Linus and the log wagon. sc17 = the hook strains, sparks at the
  wheels, the latch pops, Carter tumbles comically off the logs onto a soft sawdust pile (surprised → defeated pose).
- OWNER DECISION 2026-10-11 (overrides the two rules above, only for these moments): Carter rides ON the log wagon in sc 14/16/17
  holding the coupling CHAIN and a WHIP he cracks only IN THE AIR (crack puff + "ctar", never touching Linus); sc 17 the chain
  snaps and he tumbles onto sawdust. sc 19 James fires a cartoon shot ("DOR!", muzzle flash + white smoke from his raised hand)
  that clearly MISSES (puff in the sky / spark on a rock); Linus flinches, squashes, goes faster with extra steam. No blood, no hit,
  no injury. Exactly ONE Goro per level, emerging from and backing into a cave mouth (sc 9, 18).
- Black birds on the sign (sc9): vulture art is OK perched, not menacing.
- OWNER OVERRIDE 2026-10-12 (Malivlak mv11 only): the film's SKULL warning board on a post beside the track, at the stone tunnel arch, is allowed (cartoon, the film's own prop, drawn as inline SVG
  `.k-skull`). It overrides the "no skull" rule for this one sign; no skull anywhere else, no skull on characters or on the board's cells.
- sc24–26: Linus is hurt = "sangat lelah, penyok, uapnya habis" (sad sprite, soot, dented); no fire. sc26 = a DREAM:
  Linus floats up among soft clouds to a golden gate, light fills the screen, he wakes in the workshop (sc27).
  Never say he died.

## Immersion checklist per level (owner: "immersive ada vfx micro interaction dan semuanya")
- Weather/time matches the scenario: sun (1–7), rain + lightning (8 end – 10, 14), night (11), orange sky (15–20),
  dust (24), soft gold (26–27).
- Every story beat has a VFX moment (shared library) AND a micro-interaction: the cast reacts when Linus arrives
  (pose swap, small hop), Linus's face changes (happy/sad sprites), the caboose wobbles, birds lift off when he
  passes, the searchlight sweeps, a wreck creaks.
- Cast only where the scenario puts them (Scarlet never at the bridge; Henry not in the logging levels).
- Text short; the story card before each level states the scenario in one line.

## Lomba ke Kota (hellbent) - story, cast, scripts (owner 2026-10-12; no film source, written for this game)

gives it to whoever shows the best heart, not the best time. Theme: wait your turn, cheer a friend up, share, give way, arrive together.
